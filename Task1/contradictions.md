# Contradictions Register

Date: 2026-08-13 · Only mutually exclusive requirements are listed (both sides cannot be true at once); plain difficulties are in the [canvas risks](architecture-canvas.md#risks). Source codes: [README](../README.md#sources).

## Four things that are physically impossible together

1. **99.99% active-active multi-region with RPO = 0** and **$5,000/month for all infrastructure** — the CTO's topology alone costs ≈ $19.7k/month (C-01).
2. **40–50 self-built services, own logistics and payment orchestration** and **15 October with six contractors under a hiring freeze** — the CTO's own estimate for logistics is 6 months × 4 engineers (C-02).
3. **Nigerian personal data stays in Nigeria** and **"all infrastructure in the cloud" on AWS/GCP/Azure** — none of them has a region in Nigeria (C-03).
4. **"1 million buyers in the first month" / 10M users on day 1** and **a pilot with 100 sellers and 5,000 SKU** — 10,000 buyers per seller (C-06).

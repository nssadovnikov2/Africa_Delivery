**In one paragraph.** The investor decides date and success; the CFO holds a delegated money veto; the law outranks both. Four things are impossible together: 99.99% active-active within $5k/mo; the CTO's build-everything plan by 15 Oct with six contractors; Nigerian data in Nigeria on a hyperscaler-only cloud; and 1M buyers in month 1 with 100 sellers. We proceed on explicit defaults (99.95% SLO, partner logistics/payments, per-country PII vaults, 100k MAU sizing) until the questions are answered.
